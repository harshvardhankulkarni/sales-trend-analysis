Key Findings

  • Best day: Wednesday (Rs.76,430 avg) - likely mid-week business purchases.
  • Worst day: Monday (Rs.58,829 avg) - slowest day of the week.
  • Peak day: June 26 (Rs.97,452) - highest single-day revenue.
  • Trend: Month-over-month declined 3.3% in the final month.
  • Weekend boost: Saturday sees 50% higher sales than Monday.
